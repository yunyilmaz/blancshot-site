Privacy Policy

BlancShot · Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: your photos stay on your iPhone. BlancShot has no account, no servers that receive your photos, and no analytics or tracking.

Your photos

  • Photos you take or choose are stored only inside the app on your iPhone.
  • Background removal, edits, photo checks and exports all happen on your device. Your photos are never uploaded by BlancShot.
  • Your original photos are kept unchanged. Edits are saved as settings and applied to copies when you export.
  • Deleting a listing deletes its photos from the app. Photos you saved to your photo library stay there.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap “Take photo”, to take product photos.
  • Photos (add only): only when you tap “Save to Photos”, to add finished photos to your library. BlancShot can't read your library with this permission.
  • Choosing photos uses Apple's photo picker, which only shares the photos you pick.

No account, no tracking

  • You don't need an account. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal information.
  • BlancShot contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. The app keeps a small count of your free exports on your device (in the iOS Keychain) so the free allowance works.

Children

BlancShot isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
