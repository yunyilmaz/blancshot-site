Support

Help with BlancShot, the listing photo kit for Etsy and Shopify sellers.

Need help or found a problem? Email blancshot.app@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Will editing change my original photo?

No. Originals are always kept untouched. Brightness, warmth and crop are applied to copies when you export.

The background removal missed part of my product.

It works best with one product on a plain, contrasting surface in soft, even light. Shiny, glass or see-through items are harder. Tap Cut-out to try again, or retake the photo.

How do the free exports work?

Your first 3 complete exports are free. Failed exports don't count. After that, a Pro subscription gives unlimited exports. You can always view your photos and save your originals.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Restore: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Cancel: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Subscriptions are managed by Apple.

Are the photo checks a guarantee?

No. They check your photos against the marketplaces' published guidelines, but Etsy and Shopify make the final decision.
